Rclipper Terms of Service

Rclipper is a desktop program that runs on your own computer. You paste a source channel, it cuts short clips, and it can upload those clips to accounts you connect (YouTube, TikTok).

You are the publisher. You decide which sources to watch, which accounts to connect, and what goes public. Do not upload clips you do not have the right to post. Follow YouTube and TikTok rules.

The software is provided as-is, for your own use. There is no hosted service, no paid plan, and no warranty.
