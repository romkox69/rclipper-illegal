Rclipper Privacy Policy

Rclipper runs locally on your PC. It does not have its own cloud or user accounts.

  • Source videos, transcripts, clips, and logs stay on your disk.
  • YouTube and TikTok login tokens are stored only in a local secrets folder on your machine.
  • When you connect or publish, the app talks to Google and TikTok using those tokens. That is their servers, not ours.
  • We do not collect analytics, sell data, or run a backend that sees your clips.
